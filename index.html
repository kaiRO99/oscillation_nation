Datasets iEcodistricts: AAFC National Ecological Framework v2.2. Soil: AAFC Soil Landscapes of Canada v3.2. Crops: AAFC Annual Crop Inventory; yields: AAFC historical estimated crop yield by Soil Landscape polygon. All under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Climate: NASA POWER; recent months and the seasonal forecast (ECMWF) from Meteomatics. ENSO: NOAA CPC ONI; forecast ONI from ECMWF Niño 3.4 sea temperature (Meteomatics). Farm support payments: Statistics Canada, direct payments (province, annual). Seeded area: Statistics Canada, areas of principal field crops (Census Agricultural Regions). Irrigation: Statistics Canada, irrigation volume (every second year), by province or drainage region. Datasets are averaged per ecodistrict unless noted.

Places like yours iCompares every climate variable for the month, plus soil, with every other farmland ecodistrict in the same calendar month of other years. Forecast months are matched against past (observed) months, so you can see which places already lived through the conditions forecast for yours. 100 = identical; a random pair scores about 2.
