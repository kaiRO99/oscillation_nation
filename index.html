Datasets iEcodistricts: AAFC National Ecological Framework v2.2. Soil: AAFC Soil Landscapes of Canada v3.2. Crops: AAFC Annual Crop Inventory; yields: AAFC historical estimated crop yield by Soil Landscape polygon. All under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Climate: NASA POWER; recent months and the seasonal forecast (ECMWF) from Meteomatics. ENSO: NOAA CPC ONI; forecast ONI from ECMWF Niño 3.4 sea temperature (Meteomatics). Farm support payments: Statistics Canada, direct payments (province, annual). Seeded area: Statistics Canada, areas of principal field crops (Census Agricultural Regions) and by province. Yield forecast (end of the timeline): observed yields from the same regional table, NASA POWER weather, the ECMWF seasonal forecast and the NOAA CPC ENSO outlook; method in pipeline/build_yield_forecast.py. Irrigation: Statistics Canada, irrigation volume (every second year), by province or drainage region. Datasets are averaged per ecodistrict unless noted.

Welcome to Oscillation Nation

El Niño and La Niña shift temperature and rainfall across Canada, and every farm feels it differently. Use this map to see what past El Niño and La Niña years did to your area, and to find places that have already lived through the conditions forecast for yours.

  1. Pick a month. Drag the timeline or press ← →. Red marks El Niño, blue La Niña, green neutral. The last few months are a forecast.
  2. Choose what the map shows. Temperature or precipitation, or open Advanced for soil, crops, yields and more. Hover over the map to read the numbers.
  3. Find places like yours. Click your ecodistrict (the patch of map around your farm). Places like yours then lists the places and years whose climate and soil came closest. Click a match to zoom to it, then Generate report for a side-by-side comparison.

The i icons explain each dataset and where it comes from.
