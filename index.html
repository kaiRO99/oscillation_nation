Datasets

Ecodistricts: AAFC National Ecological Framework v2.2. Soil: AAFC Soil Landscapes of Canada v3.2. Crops: AAFC Annual Crop Inventory; yields: AAFC historical estimated crop yield by Soil Landscape polygon. All under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Climate: NASA POWER; recent months and the seasonal forecast (ECMWF) from Meteomatics. ENSO: NOAA CPC ONI; forecast ONI from ECMWF Niño 3.4 sea temperature (Meteomatics). Every dataset is averaged per ecodistrict.

Places like yours

Compares every climate variable for the month and the soil with every other farmland ecodistrict in the same calendar month of other years. Forecast months are matched against past (observed) months, so you can see which places already lived through the conditions forecast for yours. 100 = identical; a random pair scores about 2.
