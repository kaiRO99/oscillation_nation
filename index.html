Datasets

Ecodistricts: AAFC National Ecological Framework v2.2. Soil: AAFC Soil Landscapes of Canada v3.2. Crops: AAFC Annual Crop Inventory. All under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Climate: NASA POWER. ENSO: NOAA CPC ONI. Farm support payments: Statistics Canada, Direct payments and rebates (province-level, annual). Seeded area: Statistics Canada, Estimated areas of principal field crops by Small Area Data Region (Census Agricultural Regions, annual). Irrigation water use: Statistics Canada, irrigation volume by province and crop type (surveyed every second year), mapped by province or by StatCan drainage region (watershed basin). Every dataset is averaged per ecodistrict.
